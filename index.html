About the App

US Stay Tracker helps travelers monitor their physical presence in the United States. Record trips, view statistics, plan future travel, export reports to PDF or CSV, and keep your travel history organized.

Features

  • Trip Management
  • Calendar View
  • Statistics Dashboard
  • Travel Planner
  • PDF Export
  • CSV Export
  • Backup & Restore
