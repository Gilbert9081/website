About the App

US Stay Tracker helps travelers monitor their physical presence in the United States. Record trips, monitor stay limits, view statistics, plan future travel, export reports, and keep your travel history organized.

Features

  • Trip Management
  • Stay Limit Calculator
  • Travel Planner
  • Calendar View
  • Statistics Dashboard
  • PDF Export
  • CSV Export
  • Backup & Restore
