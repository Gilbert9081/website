About the App

US Stay Tracker helps travelers accurately monitor the number of days they spend in the United States. Whether you're a Canadian snowbird, business traveler, truck driver, visitor, or managing residency requirements, the app keeps all of your travel history organized in one place.

Log every entry and exit, monitor your remaining days in real time, review detailed travel statistics, browse trips on an interactive calendar, and use the built-in planner to estimate future travel before you cross the border. Export reports to PDF or CSV, back up your data, and restore it whenever needed. Designed for simplicity and accuracy, US Stay Tracker gives you confidence that your travel records are always up to date.

Features

  • Trip Management
  • Stay Limit Calculator
  • Travel Planner
  • Interactive Calendar
  • Statistics Dashboard
  • PDF & CSV Export
  • Backup & Restore
