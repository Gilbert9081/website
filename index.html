About the App

US Stay Tracker is designed for frequent travelers who need to accurately monitor the number of days they spend in the United States.

Whether you're a Canadian snowbird, business traveler, truck driver, digital nomad, tourist, or anyone who regularly crosses the U.S. border, the app helps you record every trip and stay within your permitted travel limits.

The app automatically calculates your travel days using either the B1/B2 Visa & Canadian Citizen 180-Day Planner or the ESTA / Visa Waiver Program (90 days within any rolling 180-day period).

View your travel history, monitor remaining days, plan future trips, and export reports to PDF or CSV—all while keeping your data securely stored on your device.

Features

  • Record unlimited U.S. entry and exit trips
  • Automatic travel day calculations
  • B1/B2, Canadian Citizen and ESTA support
  • Interactive calendar
  • Travel statistics dashboard
  • Future trip planner
  • PDF & CSV export
  • Backup & Restore
  • Private offline storage
