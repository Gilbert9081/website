Privacy Policy

US Stay Tracker

Last Updated: August 2026

US Stay Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Information We Store

US Stay Tracker stores only the travel information that you choose to enter, including:

  • Trip entry and exit dates
  • Travel history
  • Travel planner information
  • Application settings and preferences

All information is stored locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

US Stay Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on external servers.

The app does not:

  • Require user registration or login
  • Create user accounts
  • Collect analytics
  • Track your activity
  • Collect crash reports
  • Request or access your location
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Sell or share your information with third parties

How Your Information Is Used

The information you enter is used only within the app to:

  • Calculate U.S. travel days
  • Monitor travel limits
  • Display travel statistics
  • Plan future trips
  • Generate PDF reports
  • Generate CSV exports
  • Backup and restore your travel history (when you choose to do so)

Children's Privacy

US Stay Tracker is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Control

Because all travel information remains on your device, you maintain full control over your data.

You may delete your trips at any time or remove all stored information by uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised "Last Updated" date.
