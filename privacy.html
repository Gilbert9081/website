Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

US Stay Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, stores, and uses information.

Information We Collect

US Stay Tracker stores the trips and travel information you enter into the application. This information is stored locally on your device.

Personal Information

US Stay Tracker does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, address, or government identification.

Location

The application does not continuously track your location. Any trip information is entered manually by the user.

Data Storage

Your information remains on your device unless you choose to export or back up your data.

Third-Party Services

The application may use services provided by Google Play for app distribution, billing, and crash reporting where applicable.

Children's Privacy

US Stay Tracker is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
